REARMED 0.6.1-beta - notes for testers
======================================

Thanks for playing. What helps most, in this order:

1. DOES IT START?
   Unzip, run "Play REARMED.bat". If anything goes wrong before the main menu
   (setup can't find Quake III, a crash, a black screen), tell us what you saw.

2. PLAY ONE DESCENT FOR 30-60 MINUTES
   Main menu > DESCENT > NEW DESCENT. Play until you die or reach the first boss.
   Then tell us:
     - Was the first boss (THE BUTCHER) fair? Too easy, too hard, how many tries?
     - Did you understand what to do on each floor without reading anything?
     - What was the most fun moment? The most annoying one?
     - Did you find a vault (a secret bonus floor)? Which mode was it?

3. ANYTHING BROKEN
   A crash, a bot stuck in a wall, a menu you couldn't leave, text you couldn't
   read, a sound that's too loud.

HOW TO SEND A REPORT
   Write a few lines and attach these two files from the REARMED folder:
     home\rearmed\games.log      (what happened in the game)
     home\rearmed\qconsole.log   (errors, if there are any)
   Screenshots: F12 in game, saved in home\rearmed\screenshots\.
   Your PC: Windows version, graphics card, screen resolution.

Nothing is sent anywhere by the game. These files stay on your PC until you
send them.
